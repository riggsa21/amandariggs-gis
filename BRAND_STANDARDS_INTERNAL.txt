GEOSITE INTEL - INTERNAL WEB/CONTENT BRAND RULES

Identity
1. Always use the approved contour G (geosite-contour-g.png) in headers, footers, favicon, downloadable marketing, and branded video graphics. Never substitute a plain letter G.
2. Brand name is GeoSite Intel, LLC. GSI Workflow Studio is one service line within the company.
3. Retain the company tagline: See beyond the property line.
4. Maintain a recognizable teal/evergreen palette and a professional, geography-driven visual style.
5. Use normal short hyphens (-) in visible copy, not long em or en dashes.

Site hierarchy
6. Present GeoSite Intelligence (land/property) and GSI Workflow Studio (custom automation) as distinct services with comparable prominence.
7. About contains founder background, degrees, research, and manuscript citations. Do not add a primary Research navigation tab.
8. Portfolio contains separate sections for property research and automation. Every link goes to its actual case study or named section, not an unrelated generic page.
9. Social is the home for Rowan & Pixel, social channels, and marketing shorts. No primary Rowan & Pixel tab.
10. Do not add a public explanation of why the contour G logo is used. Brand repetition is intentional and implicit.

Accuracy
11. Label independent demos, fictional data, and research concepts clearly.
12. No unverified results, client relationships, permission claims, or download links.
13. Verify reuse rights for any new GIS imagery, maps, research manuscript files, or third-party photographs before public release.
14. All primary and project-detail pages must use the same navigation, header, footer, CSS, and footer contact information.

This file is for internal maintenance only. It is not a webpage.
