Example 01: Terrain & Drainage Context

This Frog Level / Davenport Farm corridor review demonstrates how terrain and drainage data can reveal low areas, runoff patterns, nearby roads, and conditions that may not be obvious from a listing photograph or standard parcel map.

What was reviewed

  • High-resolution LiDAR-derived terrain
  • Modeled drainage pathways and runoff direction
  • Low-area signals within the surrounding landscape
  • Road, building, and nearby land context

What it helps answer

  • How might water move through the surrounding area?
  • Where do terrain patterns suggest closer inspection?
  • Could nearby roads or drainage affect the property?
  • What questions should a buyer or owner ask next?

Decision value

The map turns several technical datasets into one readable view, helping a buyer or owner focus a property walk-through and decide whether more specialized review is appropriate.

Example 02: Road Access & Drainage Context

This North Edwards Road review examines a low roadway segment where water has been observed collecting. Terrain and contributing-area analysis add context to the access concern and identify a possible drainage bottleneck for field verification.

What was reviewed

  • High-resolution LiDAR-derived elevation
  • Modeled flow accumulation and contributing area
  • Road position and the surrounding drainage pattern
  • Expected connection to the natural flow path

Why it matters

  • Adds context to possible property-access concerns
  • Highlights conditions to verify before a purchase
  • Supports informed questions for an owner or agent
  • Helps identify the right agency or professional to contact

Decision value

Screening does not diagnose an engineering problem, but it can show how the observed location relates to the surrounding terrain and help focus the next field or professional review.
