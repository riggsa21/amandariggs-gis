This corridor review shows how terrain and drainage data can add useful context around a property—highlighting low areas, runoff patterns, nearby roads, and conditions that may not be obvious from a listing photo or standard parcel map.

Case Study 02: Access & Drainage Context — North Edwards Road

This screening examined a recurring roadway ponding location along North Edwards Road. It demonstrates how terrain and contributing-area analysis can reveal access and drainage conditions near a property and identify a likely bottleneck for closer field review.

Case Study 02 — North Edwards Road (ponding location, contributing-area signals, and likely drainage bottleneck)

What was identified

  • Observed roadway ponding along a low segment
  • Runoff routing parallel to the road shoulder
  • Contributing-area signals supporting concentrated flow
  • A possible cross-drain bottleneck to investigate
  • Expected reconnection point to the natural flow path

Why it matters

  • Adds context to year-round property access
  • Highlights conditions to verify before a purchase
  • Supports informed questions for the owner or agent
  • Provides useful documentation for follow-up conversations
  • Helps identify the right agency or professional to contact next

Data used

  • High-resolution LiDAR-derived terrain
  • Contributing area (acres) from flow accumulation
  • Road and structure layers (public sources)
  • Cartographic refinement for clear communication
