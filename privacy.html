Privacy & Data Use

This notice explains what information may be received through the GeoSite Intel website, how it is used, and which outside services help process it.

Current website setup: GeoSite Intel does not currently use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Website forms are handled through Formspree, and the website is hosted through GitHub Pages.

Information that may be collected

Information is collected when it is voluntarily submitted through a website form or email. This may include a name, email address, phone number, property location, property or parcel number, listing link, project questions, intended property use, budget range, and deadlines.

Formspree processes website form submissions and may receive routine technical information used for security and delivery, such as an IP address, browser type, access time, and referring website address. GitHub Pages may also process ordinary hosting and security logs.

If a document is needed after the first consultation, GeoSite may request it by email. Documents should not include Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, medical information, passwords, or other sensitive personal information that is not needed for the property question.

Information submitted through the site is used to respond to the request, evaluate whether the work fits GeoSite services, prepare a plan and estimate, communicate about the project, deliver authorized work, and prevent misuse.

How information is handled

Information is limited to the uses connected with the inquiry, project, website security, recordkeeping, and applicable legal obligations.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold. It may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, email, and other necessary business functions.

Retention

Inquiry and project information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, business records, dispute handling, security, and legal requirements.

Choices

Questions, correction requests, or deletion requests may be sent by email. Some records may need to be retained when required for legal, accounting, security, or contractual purposes.

Cookies and outside services

The current GeoSite Intel pages do not contain Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising trackers, or other site-added analytics cookies. Hosting, security, and form providers may use technical measures needed to operate and protect their services under their own privacy policies.

If analytics, advertising, payment, scheduling, or other tools are added later, this notice and any required consent controls should be updated.

Privacy questions

Questions about this notice or information submitted to GeoSite Intel can be sent to info@geositeintel.com.
