Environmental Due Diligence & Compliance Support

GeoSite Intel, LLC helps environmental consultants, property professionals, developers, lenders, and technology teams organize location-based evidence, map regulatory context, review historical conditions, and pressure-test automated property outputs before decisions rely on them.

Request Due Diligence SupportView Property Examples
Scope matters. GeoSite can support formal environmental due diligence with GIS, research, mapping, and QA/QC. GeoSite does not represent this screening work as a formal Phase I Environmental Site Assessment or an Environmental Professional opinion.

Where GeoSite can support the due diligence workflow

The goal is not to replace the qualified professional. It is to make the spatial evidence easier to find, check, understand, and document.

Regulatory & Environmental Screening

Map publicly available environmental records, nearby regulated facilities, mapped hazards, and other property-specific environmental context that may warrant deeper review.

Historical Site Intelligence

Review historical aerial imagery, maps, land-cover change, surrounding development, and other available evidence to identify changes or past uses worth investigating.

Phase I ESA GIS Support

Provide map exhibits, adjoining-property context, record geolocation, distance checks, parcel verification, and spatial research to support qualified environmental professionals conducting formal assessments.

Development Compliance Screening

Organize mapped floodplain, wetlands, buffers, protected resources, zoning/land-use context, and known permitting considerations as an early screen before agency or professional verification.

Automated GIS & Report QA-QC

Pressure-test automated outputs for wrong parcels, bad joins, duplicate or missing records, questionable distances, inconsistent maps, unsupported language, source gaps, and other errors that can hide behind a polished report.

Custom Spatial Research

Build a focused research workflow for environmental, property, lending, consulting, or PropTech questions that require defensible spatial context and traceable sources.

Human review for automated property intelligence

Automation is powerful. It still needs spatial QA.

A system can generate a professional-looking report while using the wrong parcel, an outdated source, a bad coordinate, an incorrect buffer, or a misleading interpretation. GeoSite can independently review the geospatial logic and evidence behind the output.

Geometry & Location

Confirm parcel identity, coordinate placement, CRS/datum, spatial joins, buffers, distances, and map alignment.

Data & Completeness

Check source vintage, coverage, duplicate/missing records, data gaps, and whether the selected datasets actually support the question.

Language & Evidence

Flag statements that go beyond what the mapped or regulatory evidence can establish and distinguish screening flags from final determinations.

Clear boundaries around the work

GeoSite reports what the available evidence supports and identifies when a qualified professional or agency should take over.

GeoSite screening does not itself constitute a formal Phase I Environmental Site Assessment, Environmental Professional opinion, engineering determination, survey, jurisdictional wetland delineation, legal opinion, permit determination, regulatory approval, or guarantee of compliance/buildability. When a project requires one of those services, GeoSite's role is limited to agreed-upon GIS, research, mapping, screening, and QA/QC support unless an appropriately qualified professional is separately engaged.
