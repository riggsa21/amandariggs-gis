What would you like to stop doing by hand?

A short email is enough to begin. No call is required for the initial review.

  1. What do you do manually today?
  2. How often do you do it, and roughly how long does it take?
  3. What file types and software are involved?
  4. What should the finished result look like?
  5. Is there a deadline or budget range?

Start with a description or a small anonymized example. GeoSite will confirm which sample files are needed for a scope review.
