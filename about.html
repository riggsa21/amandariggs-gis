About GeoSite Intel

Focused GIS support with the reasoning left intact.

GeoSite Intel LLC is a small geospatial practice built to help land development and consulting teams complete defined GIS work with clear methods, careful QA, professional maps, and usable handoffs.

Discuss a projectReview completed work
Working principle

See beyond the property line.

A useful site analysis connects the subject property to its terrain, drainage, surrounding land, source quality, and the decision the project team is trying to make.

Founder

Amanda L. Riggs

Amanda is a geographer, GIS analyst, and published researcher with an M.S. in Geography. Her work combines spatial analysis, environmental and planning context, cartography, data QA, QGIS, Python, SQL, and technical documentation.

GeoSite was created to turn those skills into practical project support - especially when a development or consulting team has a specific GIS assignment but limited internal capacity to complete it.

DEVDevelopment context
Parcel, environmental, terrain, drainage, land-use, and conceptual scenario analysis.
A clearer company direction

Specialized enough to be useful.

GeoSite is not presented as three different companies or a general research shop. The commercial focus is one service line: development GIS project support.

Who

Project teams

Land developers, acquisition teams, civil and environmental consultants, planners, and small firms needing outside GIS capacity.

What

Defined assignments

Project data setup, site and constraint analysis, terrain/drainage screening, map production, and focused workflow automation.

How

Documented delivery

A written scope, agreed inputs and outputs, review points, data and method notes, and a clear handoff by email.
