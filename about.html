Background

Research habits meet hands-on technical work.

GeoSite Intel was founded by Amanda L. Riggs, who holds an M.S. in Geography. Her work includes GIS, spatial analysis, Python scripting, remote sensing, professional cartography, environmental research, and technical reporting.

GeoSite Intelligence applies those skills to land and site questions. GSI Workflow Studio develops reusable tools for data-heavy, repetitive work.

The two services share a commitment to documented inputs, quality checks, honest limitations, and clear handoffs.

Assessing Nutrient Export and Stormwater Retention Amid Urban Growth in Greenville, North Carolina

Amanda Riggs, Steven M. Richter, and Beth Bee

Southeastern Geographer, 66(2), 122-142 (2026).

The study used land-use and land-cover data and ecosystem-service modeling to examine relationships among urban growth, nutrient export, and stormwater retention in Greenville-area subwatersheds.

From research to useful methods

The paper's emphasis on landscape change, stormwater retention, and limits of geographic information informs GeoSite's interest in site-level scenario analysis and transparent environmental screening.
