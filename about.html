About GeoSite Intel

GeoSite Intel is an independent GIS and environmental mapping service created by Amanda L. Riggs to help people understand land and property before making important decisions.

A listing, tax card, or standard parcel viewer can only tell part of the story. I bring together relevant parcel records, terrain, environmental data, historical imagery, and surrounding land context to create focused research for buyers, property owners, real estate professionals, and small investors.

  • ▸Parcel & location research
  • ▸Environmental & terrain screening
  • ▸Historical imagery review
  • ▸Property comparison
  • ▸Professional custom maps
  • ▸Flexible GIS project support

Service note: GeoSite provides GIS-based research and screening—not a boundary survey, title opinion, appraisal, engineering design, legal review, or formal environmental assessment.

Meet Amanda

I’m Amanda L. Riggs, a geographer and GIS analyst based in Eastern North Carolina. I hold an M.S. in Geography and a GIS certificate, with experience in spatial analysis, remote sensing, environmental modeling, LiDAR terrain analysis, and professional cartography.

My graduate research examined how urban growth affects environmental conditions using land-cover change, watershed analysis, and ecosystem-service modeling. That work contributed to peer-reviewed research on urban growth, nutrient delivery, and stormwater retention. GeoSite allows me to apply the same careful, evidence-based approach to practical property and land questions.

I believe useful GIS work should do more than produce an attractive map. It should answer a real question, explain the limits of the available information, and help someone decide what to investigate next.

How I approach property research

Every project begins with the decision you are trying to make. From there, I identify the most relevant available data and build a focused review rather than delivering a generic stack of maps.

Decision first

The scope is built around your property question, priorities, and intended use.

Evidence together

Parcel, terrain, environmental, and imagery sources are combined so relationships are easier to see.

Clear next steps

You receive professional maps, plain-language findings, stated limitations, and useful follow-up questions.

Have a property question?

Send the address or parcel number and tell me what you are trying to understand. I’ll reply with the available data, recommended scope, turnaround, and an estimate.
