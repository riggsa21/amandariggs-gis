About GeoSite Intel LLC

Practical tools.
Grounded in careful analysis.

GeoSite Intel combines geography, programming, data quality, and workflow design to help teams use their information more consistently.

Why this direction?

Good systems should make everyday work lighter.

GeoSite Intel started with spatial data analysis, environmental research, and project-based GIS work. Over time, the same challenge kept appearing across projects: valuable staff hours lost to repeated file handling, data cleaning, QA, and reporting steps.

Today the primary service is practical task automation and custom Python workflows. GIS and land-analysis support remains an area of technical expertise, with research continuing as a separate long-term pursuit.

How GeoSite approaches a build

  • Understand the real task before recommending software.
  • Protect source files and document assumptions.
  • Use clear inputs and reviewable outputs.
  • Flag exceptions rather than hiding uncertainty.
  • Agree ownership, setup, testing, and handoff in writing.
Founder and technical background

Geography meets programming.

GeoSite founder Amanda L. Riggs holds a graduate degree in Geography and has a background in GIS analysis, Python scripting, spatial research, and professional mapping. Her co-authored 2026 study addressed land-use change, stormwater retention, and nutrient export.

The same evidence-minded approach now supports repeatable business processes, data validation, clear technical documentation, and tailored scripts.
