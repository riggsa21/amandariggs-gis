About GeoSite Intel

GeoSite Intel is an independent GIS and environmental mapping service created by Amanda L. Riggs to help people understand land and property before making important decisions.

A listing, tax card, or standard parcel viewer can only tell part of the story. I bring together relevant parcel records, terrain, environmental data, historical imagery, and surrounding land context to create focused research for buyers, owners, real estate professionals, and small investors.

  • Parcel and location research
  • Environmental and terrain screening
  • Historical imagery review
  • Property comparison
  • Professional custom maps
  • Flexible GIS project support
Service note: GeoSite provides GIS-based research, screening, and mapping—not a boundary survey, title opinion, appraisal, engineering design, legal review, or formal environmental assessment.

Meet Amanda

I’m Amanda L. Riggs, a geographer and GIS analyst based in Eastern North Carolina. I hold an M.S. in Geography and a GIS certificate, with experience in spatial analysis, remote sensing, environmental modeling, LiDAR terrain analysis, and professional cartography.

My graduate research examined how urban growth affects environmental conditions using land-cover change, watershed analysis, and ecosystem-service modeling. That work contributed to peer-reviewed research involving urban growth, nutrient delivery, and stormwater retention.

GeoSite allows me to apply the same careful, evidence-based approach to practical property and land questions while presenting the results in a format that is understandable to non-technical clients.

Useful GIS work should do more than produce an attractive map. It should answer a real question, explain the limits of the available information, and help someone decide what to investigate next.

How I Approach Property Research

Every project begins with the decision you are trying to make. From there, I identify the most relevant available data and build a focused review rather than delivering a generic stack of maps.

Decision First

The scope is built around your property question, priorities, and intended use.

Evidence Together

Parcel, terrain, environmental, and imagery sources are combined so important relationships are easier to recognize.

Clear Next Steps

You receive professional maps, plain-language findings, stated limitations, and useful follow-up questions.
