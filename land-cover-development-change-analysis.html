Change should be measured against consistent definitions.

Existing GeoSite demonstrations compared 2001 to 2024 land-cover patterns using documented annual land-cover data. The analysis considered surrounding development expansion as part of broader watershed interpretation.

Differences in source dates, classification, pixel scale, and change thresholds are important to disclose. Outputs are land-cover comparisons, not evidence of a permitted or unpermitted activity.
