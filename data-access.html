Research idea / Data access

Good analysis begins with usable data.

GIS datasets can be discoverable but unavailable for a particular commercial or publishing use. GeoSite is exploring a checklist-driven approach for identifying access limits early.

A concept under exploration

Questions to answer before mapping.

Availability

Is there coverage for the geography, date, and resolution required?

License and attribution

What use, transformation, derivative works, distribution, and citation requirements apply?

Fitness and uncertainty

Are the projection, collection method, age, completeness, and limitations suitable for the question?

This is an exploratory research concept, not a deployed screening tool or a legal licensing opinion.
