Independent Demonstration Project 01

Terrain & Drainage Context — Frog Level / Davenport Farm

This corridor-level review demonstrates how high-resolution terrain, modeled runoff patterns, roads, buildings, and surrounding land context can reveal conditions that may not be obvious in listing photographs or a standard parcel viewer.

The property question

Where are the strongest low-area and concentrated-flow signals, and which locations deserve a closer walk-through?

What was reviewed

LiDAR-derived elevation, terrain patterns, modeled water flow, roads, buildings, and the surrounding landscape.

How it helps

Gives a buyer or owner focused locations to inspect and better drainage questions to ask before moving forward.

Independent Demonstration Project 02

Road Access & Drainage Context — North Edwards Road

This focused review examines a low roadway segment where water has been observed collecting. It shows how terrain and contributing-area data can add context to an access concern and identify a possible drainage bottleneck for field verification.

The property question

Why might water collect along this road segment, and how does the surrounding terrain direct water toward it?

What was reviewed

Elevation, modeled flow accumulation, road position, surrounding drainage, and the expected connection to a natural flow path.

How it helps

Adds context to possible access concerns and supports better questions for an owner, agent, road authority, or drainage professional.

What Your Property Review May Include

Every project is scoped around your actual question. Depending on the property and available data, a review may include the following.

Parcel & Surrounding Context

Public parcel information, nearby land uses, roads, water features, development patterns, and other relevant surroundings.

Terrain & Drainage Screening

Elevation, slope, low areas, modeled flow paths, contributing area, and terrain conditions that deserve closer field review.

Flood, Wetland & Soil Context

Publicly available flood, wetland, soil, watershed, and water-resource information organized around the property question.

Historical Change

Historical aerial photographs, satellite imagery, land-cover change, and visible changes to access, drainage, or nearby development.

Clear Maps & Written Findings

A readable map and concise explanation of the relevant evidence, data sources, limitations, and suggested next questions.

Custom Comparison

Side-by-side research for multiple parcels when you need to compare terrain, environmental context, access, or surrounding conditions.

How It Works

A simple process designed around the decision you are trying to make.

Share the property

Send the address, parcel number, listing link, coordinates, or a map showing the land you want researched.

Explain your question

Tell me what you are considering and what concerns you—such as drainage, access, historical change, or comparing several parcels.

Receive focused findings

I review the available information, explain what it can and cannot show, and provide the agreed-upon maps or research summary.

Clear Scope, Clear Limitations

Good property research should help you ask better questions without overstating what public data can prove.

GeoSite Intel provides GIS-based research, screening, and mapping. Services do not replace a boundary survey, title search, appraisal, environmental site assessment, engineering evaluation, legal advice, official flood determination, permitting review, or on-site inspection. When the evidence points to an issue outside GeoSite’s scope, the report will identify the appropriate next question or type of professional review.
