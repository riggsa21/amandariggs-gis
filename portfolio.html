Independent Demonstration Project 01

Terrain & Drainage Context - Frog Level / Davenport Farm

This corridor-level review demonstrates how detailed elevation, computer-modeled runoff paths, roads, buildings, and surrounding land context can reveal conditions that may not be obvious in listing photographs or a standard parcel viewer.

Independent Demonstration Project 02

Road Access & Drainage Context - North Edwards Road

This focused review examines a low roadway segment where water has been observed collecting. It shows how terrain and the uphill drainage area can add context to an access concern and identify a possible drainage bottleneck for an on-site check.

The Property Question

Why might water collect along this road segment, and how does the surrounding terrain direct water toward it?

What Was Reviewed

Elevation, places where water may concentrate, road position, surrounding drainage, and the expected connection to a natural flow path.

How It Helps

Adds context to possible access concerns and supports better questions for an owner, agent, road authority, or drainage professional.

What Your Property Review May Include

Every project is planned around the actual question. Depending on the property and available data, a review may include the following.

Parcel & Surrounding Context

Public parcel information, nearby land uses, roads, water features, development patterns, and other relevant surroundings.

Early Terrain & Drainage Review

Elevation, slope, low areas, modeled water paths, the area draining toward the site, and land conditions that deserve closer field review.

Flood, Wetland & Soil Context

Publicly available flood, wetland, soil, drainage-area, and water information organized around the property question.

Historical Change

Past aerial photographs, satellite images, changes in vegetation, and visible changes to access, drainage, or nearby development.

Clear Maps & Written Findings

A readable map and concise explanation of the relevant evidence, data sources, limitations, and suggested next questions.

Custom Comparison

Side-by-side research for multiple parcels when you need to compare terrain, environmental context, access, or surrounding conditions.

How It Works

A simple process designed around the decision you are trying to make.

Share the Property

Send the address, parcel number, listing link, coordinates, or a map showing the land you want researched.

Explain Your Question

Describe what you are considering and what concerns you - such as drainage, access, historical change, or comparing several parcels.

Receive Focused Findings

GeoSite reviews the available information, explains what it can and cannot show, and provides the agreed-upon maps or research summary.

Clear Work, Clear Limitations

Good property research should help you ask better questions without overstating what public data can prove.

GeoSite Intel provides map-based property research and early review. Services do not replace a boundary survey, title search, appraisal, environmental site assessment, engineering evaluation, legal advice, official flood determination, permitting review, or on-site inspection. When the findings point to an issue outside GeoSite’s services, the report will identify the appropriate next question or type of professional review.
