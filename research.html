GeoSite Intel's technical foundations include peer-reviewed geographic research on stormwater retention, nutrient export, and urban development in eastern North Carolina.

Peer-reviewed publication / 2026

Assessing Nutrient Export and Stormwater Retention Amid Urban Growth in Greenville, North Carolina

Amanda Riggs, Steven M. Richter, and Beth Bee. Southeastern Geographer, 66(2), 122–142 (2026).

The study examined ecosystem-service changes in five Greenville-area subwatersheds between 2001 and 2021 using land-cover data and InVEST modeling. The analysis considered stormwater retention and nutrient export as urbanization transformed local landscapes.
