What the research changes in practice.

The value is not a research label. It is the discipline to make spatial claims traceable, proportionate to the data, and useful to the next reviewer.

Methods

Reproducible analysis

Inputs, transformations, assumptions, coordinate systems, and processing decisions are documented so results can be reviewed.

Context

Beyond a parcel outline

Development questions are interpreted within terrain, soils, drainage networks, land cover, and the surrounding landscape.

Limits

Uncertainty stays visible

Screening evidence is separated from official determinations, field verification, surveyed conditions, and engineering conclusions.
