How projects begin

A small, reviewable first scope.

The fastest route to a useful engagement is one representative assignment with known inputs and a clear definition of done.

Define the question

What must the project team decide, review, submit, map, or repeat?

Review the evidence

What data already exist, what must be sourced, and which gaps affect confidence?

Agree on the handoff

Which maps, datasets, tables, scripts, notes, file formats, and review points are required?

Professional boundaries

GIS support that complements the project team.

GeoSite provides spatial analysis, data preparation, planning-level screening, mapping, and workflow support. Findings are documented with their source, assumptions, and limitations so they can be used appropriately.

Separate verification may be required

GeoSite does not provide boundary surveys, stamped engineering, drainage design, geotechnical conclusions, jurisdictional wetland determinations, legal opinions, permit approval, or guarantees of buildability. Assignments can help focus the questions sent to the appropriate professional or authority.

Include four things in the first email.

Project location or study area. Files and links already available. The output your team needs. The deadline or next review date.
