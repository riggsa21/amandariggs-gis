Current services

Fix the data before it becomes the next problem.

Projects are scoped around a defined dataset and a defined output - not open-ended clerical work.

XLS

Spreadsheet & CSV Cleanup

Repair inconsistent columns, labels, formats, missing values, duplicates, and structural issues that make tabular data difficult to use.

MERGE

Dataset Consolidation

Combine multiple spreadsheets, exports, or tables into a consistent structure with clear field mapping and documented assumptions.

QA

Data Quality & Validation

Identify duplicates, invalid values, inconsistent categories, gaps, formatting problems, and other quality issues before analysis or reporting.

STD

Field & Format Standardization

Standardize dates, categories, identifiers, units, field names, and common data types so datasets behave consistently.

META

Metadata & Data Documentation

Create data dictionaries, field definitions, source notes, naming conventions, and other documentation that makes a dataset easier to understand and maintain.

READY

Analysis-Ready Preparation

Prepare clean inputs for GIS, dashboards, databases, reporting, modeling, or automation workflows without changing the underlying business meaning of the data.

How it works

Define the rules. Clean the data. Document the result.

1

Review a sample

Share a representative dataset and explain what is wrong, inconsistent, difficult to combine, or preventing the next step.

2

Define the target structure

We agree on the cleanup rules, field mapping, output format, validation checks, and what should remain unchanged.

3

Transform, validate & document

GSI returns the cleaned dataset along with the agreed documentation, QA notes, or data dictionary needed to understand the result.

Good fit for Data Intelligence

  • Inconsistent Excel or CSV datasets
  • Multiple exports that need to be combined
  • Research, consulting, operational, or GIS datasets that need QA
  • Data that needs standard fields, categories, dates, identifiers, or units
  • Inputs that must be prepared for dashboards, databases, GIS, reporting, or automation

Outside this service

  • Bookkeeping, accounting, tax preparation, or financial reconciliation
  • Receipt or invoice categorization and routine expense entry
  • Open-ended manual data entry or virtual-assistant work
  • General administrative filing or routine digital file sorting
  • Physical document handling
Data-focused boundary. GSI can clean and structure data fields, but does not make accounting classifications, financial judgments, or tax decisions.
