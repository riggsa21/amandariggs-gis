GSI Website Update - V5
September 18, 2026

PRIMARY CHANGE
Data Intelligence has been tightened to a professional data-focused service.

Data Intelligence now emphasizes:
- Spreadsheet and CSV cleanup
- Dataset consolidation
- Data quality and validation
- Field and format standardization
- Metadata and data dictionaries
- Analysis-ready preparation for GIS, dashboards, databases, reporting, modeling, and automation

Explicitly outside the service:
- Bookkeeping or accounting
- Tax preparation
- Receipt/invoice categorization
- Routine expense entry
- Open-ended manual data entry
- Virtual-assistant/admin filing work
- Physical document handling

PRICING POSITIONING
Low fixed-price clerical items were removed. Data Intelligence now uses project-based scoping after sample review so pricing can reflect technical complexity, volume, validation, and deliverables.

- GeoSite pages retain their service-specific navigation.
- Service-card artwork remains uncropped.
