Send a property. Start with the question.

A full research plan is not needed to get started. Share the property, intended use, and the questions behind the decision. GeoSite Intel can review the request, identify useful available data, and recommend a focused plan of work before the project begins.

1. Share the Location

An address, property number, listing link, map location, county property-map link, or image can identify the property.

2. Explain the Goal

Describe the intended use, purchase decision, development idea, concern, or comparison that needs research.

3. Add What Is Known

Include useful links, documents, maps, photographs, deadlines, or previous findings when available.

Projects are not limited to a local service area. Remote research can be considered across locations where suitable data are available. Field visits may be added when on-site observations would strengthen the project.
