Methods and limitations

Map the relationship, then check the assumptions.

GeoSite workflows have included DEM review, drainage derivation, flow accumulation, catchment context, and downstream relationships for GIS screening. Terrain resolution, flow-routing assumptions, existing infrastructure, and field conditions all affect interpretation.

Simple raster drainage traces are not a substitute for engineered stormwater or hydraulic modeling. In low-relief areas, subtle elevation error can make apparent routes unreliable.
